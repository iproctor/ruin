# Ruin: Gods of Chaos — Rules

## Overview

Four Chaos Gods compete to corrupt and dominate the mortal world. Each player takes the role of one of the four **Chaos Powers** (also called **Powers** or **Gods**).

There are two routes to victory:

- **Victory Points (VP):** All four Powers are rewarded for corrupting and dominating regions of the world map. Rewards are tracked on the **Victory Point Track**. If any Power has 50 or more VP at the end of a game round, the game ends.
- **Threat Dial:** Each Power has a unique way to impose its **Threat** on the world. Each Power must advance its **Threat Dial** to a certain point to win by this method. One unit of dial advancement is called a **Tick**.

If no Power has won by the time the World Deck is exhausted, the mortal world is the victor and all players lose.

- An area for placing follower figures and World tokens

### The Threat Dials

Each Threat Dial has:
- A **main window** showing the current instruction/benefit for that Power
- A smaller **Threat window** showing the current numerical **Threat value** for that Power

The colored track around each dial indicates how far it must advance to reach **Victory**.

### Victory Point Track

A numbered track where each player's Victory Point Marker records their running total. The track length is not a cap — use any convenient method if markers go beyond the printed track.

### World Deck and Track

The **World Deck** is placed facedown. A **World Track** has two spaces ("1" and "2") where active World Cards are placed faceup.

### Ruination Cards

Five ordered cards (numbered 1–5) stored in a stack on the board. They are placed on regions when those regions become ruined.

## Setup

1. **Place Game Board.** Set each Threat Dial to its "Start" position.
2. **Distribute Power Sheets and Arrange Seating.** Assign Powers by consensus or randomly. If fewer than four players, unused Powers are removed. Seat players in Power Order (clockwise).
3. **Distribute Components.** Give each player their Power's markers, corruption tokens, Chaos cards, upgrade cards, and followers. Tuck unused upgrade cards under the edge of each Power Sheet.
4. **Place Markers.** Each player places their Power Marker on the highlighted starting space of the power point track on their Power Sheet, and their Victory Point Marker on "0" on the Victory Point Track.
5. **Prepare Shared Components.** Sort dial advancement counters and World Tokens into separate piles accessible to all players. Sort the Ruination Cards with "1" on top and "5" on the bottom and place on the board.
6. **Create World Deck.** Shuffle all World Cards. Deal out a facedown deck of **7 cards** (4-player game) or **8 cards** (3-player game). Return unused cards to the box.
7. **Place Starting World Tokens.** Separate 2 Noble tokens, 3 Chaos Stone tokens, and 4 Peasant tokens (9 total). Randomly place one token in each of the nine regions in Region Order.
8. **Shuffle and Draw Chaos Cards.** Each player shuffles their Chaos deck and draws a starting hand of **3 cards**.

## The Game Round

Each round consists of six phases in order:

1. World Phase
2. Draw Phase
3. Summoning Phase
4. Battle Phase
5. Corruption Phase
6. End Phase

---

## 1. World Phase

Draw the top card from the World Deck and immediately carry out its **italicized instructions**. Decisions required by the card are made by the player with the **lowest Threat**.

After resolving immediate instructions (unless the card says to discard it):
- Place the card faceup in **space "1"** of the World Track.
- Any card previously in space "1" moves to space "2."
- Any card previously in space "2" is removed from play and returned to the box.

Cards with **"Discard this card instead of adding it to the World Track"** are removed after resolving. Cards already on the track are unaffected.

### Twin-Tailed Comet Icon

Some World Cards bear a **Twin-Tailed Comet** icon. When a World Card that instructs **"Remove any World Cards bearing the Twin-Tailed Comet icon from the World Track"** enters play, all such marked cards are immediately removed from the track before the new card is added.

---

## 2. Draw Phase

Each player:
- Draws 2 Chaos cards from their own deck. Exception: Tenzen draws up to 5 (see below).
- Resets their Power Marker to their starting power points for the round (or higher if upgrade cards grant bonus points).

Starting power points by Power:

| Power | Base Power Points |
|---|---|
| Xarm | 7 |
| Gormen | 6 |
| Tenzen | 6 |
| Lasha | 6 |

If a player's Chaos deck is ever exhausted, they shuffle their discard pile to form a new deck.

There is **no hand size limit**.

Players may act **simultaneously** during the draw phase.

**Tenzen special rule:** Tenzen draws up to 5 cards in the draw phase (or 6 with the Flood of Magic upgrade), bringing their hand to that total rather than simply adding 2. Tenzen may discard 1 card from hand before drawing.

Power points are **never carried over** between rounds.

---

## 3. Summoning Phase

Players take turns in **Power Order**, cycling repeatedly (Xarm → Gormen → Tenzen → Lasha → Xarm → …).

On each turn, a player may either:
- **Summon** one follower figure, or
- **Play** one Chaos card.

Both actions require spending power points. To spend power points, move the Power Marker left by the cost amount.

If a player does not wish to act on their turn, they must move their Power Marker to the **"0" space**, ending their participation for this phase.

If a player's Power Marker is on "0" when their turn comes, their turn is **skipped**.

The Summoning Phase ends when **all players' Power Markers are on "0."**

### Summoning Follower Figures

To summon a follower:
1. Choose one of your available figures (from reserve or from the board).
2. Pay the figure's cost.
3. Place the figure in a legal region.

**Placement restriction:** A figure must be placed in a region where you already have at least one figure, or in a region **adjacent** to such a region. Exception: if you have no figures on the board, your first figure may be placed in any region.

If you pick up your last figure from a region to summon it elsewhere, you may still count that region as "occupied by your figure" for the purpose of determining where you can place the summoned figure.

### Playing Chaos Cards

To play a Chaos card:
1. Choose a card from your hand.
2. Pay the card's cost.
3. Place the card in an **empty card space** on any region and carry out its effects.

Each region has two card spaces. If both are filled, no cards may be played there. If all 18 board card spaces are full, no Chaos card can be played.

When two Chaos cards played to the same region would resolve simultaneously, the **left-hand (eastern) card** resolves first.

**Card effects are mandatory** unless the card text says otherwise, assuming the effect is possible.

Effects of two copies of the same card in the same region are generally cumulative, unless the effect is obviously non-cumulative by nature.

### Chaos Card Anatomy

- **Title:** The name of the card.
- **Cost:** Power points to play. Also the card's contribution to domination value in its region (this value is never modified by other effects).
- **Magic Symbol** *(if present):* The card contributes one magic symbol to the region while it remains there.
- **Effect:** The special game effect ("text effect") of the card. "You" refers to the player who played it.

---

## 4. Battle Phase

Battle is carried out **region by region** in standard Region Order. It occurs in every region where at least one player is entitled to roll battle dice and has at least one legal target.

For each region, each player (in Power Order) does the following:
1. **Calculate battle dice:** Sum the attack values of all their figures in the region. Roll that many dice plus any additional dice from card effects.
2. **Assign hits:** For each die result of **4, 5, or 6**, score one **hit**. Every result of **6 explodes** — roll one additional die immediately (which can hit and/or explode again, with no limit).

### Assigning Hits

Hits must be assigned to **enemy figures or Peasant tokens** in the same region. A player may not assign hits to their own figures. Excess hits (no legal targets) are lost.

A player **must** assign hits if legal targets exist, even if unfavorable.

A player **may not assign fewer hits** to a figure than required to kill it in order to "store" remaining hits and combine them with another player's hits. Players cannot gang up on a single figure.

If enough hits are assigned to a figure to equal or exceed its **defense value**, that figure is **tipped** to indicate it is killed. Tipped figures remain on the board until all players in that region have rolled.

Once all players in a region have rolled and assigned hits, all tipped figures are **removed from the board**. Then the next region is addressed.

**Peasant tokens** are an exception: they can sustain only **one hit** and are removed **immediately** when hit (placed on the killing player's Power Sheet). They are not tipped.

### Beginning of Battle / End of Battle Effects

Certain effects (from Chaos cards, etc.) trigger "at the beginning of battle" or "at the end of battle." These occur **before any player rolls normal battle dice in any region**, or **after all players have rolled all dice in all regions**, respectively.

Figures killed by beginning-of-battle effects are **removed immediately** rather than tipped, meaning they cannot roll their own dice. However, hits from beginning-of-battle effects are cumulative with regular hits rolled by the same player.

---

## 5. Corruption Phase

The Corruption Phase has two steps, in order:
1. **Domination Step**
2. **Corruption Step**

### Step 1: Domination Step

For each region (in Region Order), calculate each player's **Domination Value**:

> **Domination Value = Sum of Chaos Card Costs (played in that region) + Quantity of figures (of any class) in that region**

Note: Only the **quantity** of figures counts, not their summoning costs. A Incarnation counts the same as a Cultist.

Note: Use the **printed cost** of each Chaos card, not the actual amount paid.

The player with the **highest Domination Value** compares it to the region's **Resistance**. If it **strictly exceeds** the Resistance (equal does not count), that player scores **VP equal to the region's Conquest Value**.

If two or more players **tie** for highest Domination Value, **no one** dominates and no VP are scored, even if the tied value exceeded the Resistance.

**Ruined regions are skipped** for domination (no VP are scored).

### Step 2: Corruption Step

For each region (in Region Order), each player places **one corruption token** in the region for each of their **Cultists** present.

After placing tokens, count the **total corruption tokens** in the region across all players. **Chaos Stone tokens** also count as corruption tokens for this total.

If the total is **12 or more**, the region is **ruined**: place the next available Ruination Card (by order number) on the region.

Each player who placed **at least one corruption token during this step** immediately scores the VP indicated on the ruination card as "Ruiners Score." Players who have tokens from previous rounds but placed none this step do not score ruiner points.

If 5 regions are already ruined (no ruination cards remain), any region that would otherwise be ruined is not ruined, and no ruination points are scored.

**Ruined regions are skipped** — no corruption tokens are placed.

---

## 6. End Phase

The End Phase has these steps in order:

1. Remove Chaos Cards from the Board
2. Resolve Hero Tokens
3. Resolve World Cards
4. Score Ruined Regions
5. Advance Threat Dials
6. Check for Game End

### Step 1: Remove Chaos Cards

Each player removes all their played Chaos cards from the board and places them in their discard pile.

### Step 2: Resolve Hero Tokens

In each region containing a **Hero token**, the player with the **greatest Threat** who also has at least one figure in that region must choose one of their figures in that region and **remove it from the board**.

This is carried out once per Hero token, one at a time. If a player's last figure is eliminated, subsequent tokens in the same region affect remaining players.

### Step 3: Resolve World Cards

Examine the World Card Track. Resolve any effects beginning with **"When World Cards are resolved…"** in track order (space "1" first, then space "2").

Some World Cards have **constant** effects ("While this card remains in play…") or trigger at other specified times. If no timing is specified, the effect resolves in this step.

### Step 4: Score Ruined Regions

For each Ruination Card placed **this round** (still faceup), score points based on corruption token counts in the ruined region (in Region Order):

- The player with the **most** corruption tokens scores the **"first" value** from the ruination card for that region.
- The player with the **second-most** corruption tokens scores the **"second" value**.
- If only one player has tokens, only the "first" value is scored.
- **Ties for first:** Sum the "first" and "second" values, divide by the number of tied players (round down). No other players score.
- **Ties for second:** Divide the "second" value by the number of tied players (round down).

After scoring, the Ruination Card is turned **facedown**, permanently marking the region as ruined. All corruption tokens are returned to their players' stockpiles.

### Step 5: Advance Threat Dials

Each player who has **one or more dial advancement counters** on their dial advances it by **one tick** clockwise and carries out the newly revealed instruction.

Additionally, the player with the **most advancement counters** advances their dial by **one additional tick**. If two or more players are tied for the most counters, no additional tick is awarded.

After all advancements, **all advancement counters are returned to the stockpile**.

### Step 6: Check for Game End

Check the following conditions **in order**. The game ends as soon as one is true:

1. **Maximum Threat:** One or more players' dials show "Victory!" → That player wins. Ties broken by VP; further ties are shared victories.
2. **50 Victory Points:** One or more players have 50+ VP → The player with the most VP wins. Ties broken by highest Threat.
3. **Five Regions Ruined:** Five regions are ruined → The player with the most VP wins. Ties broken by highest Threat.
4. **World Deck Exhausted:** No cards remain in the World Deck → All players lose.

If none of these are true, begin the next round with the World Phase.

## Threat Dials

### Threat Value

The smaller window on each Threat Dial shows that Power's **Threat** — a number used to break ties and determine priority in certain situations (e.g., who controls the World Phase, who Hero tokens target).

### Dial Advancement Conditions

Each Power has a unique **Dial Advancement Condition** listed on its Power Sheet. Each time that condition is fulfilled, the player immediately places one **Dial Advancement Counter** on their Threat Dial.

A single event cannot trigger **multiple** advancement counters, even if the triggering quantity is in excess. For example, killing four enemies in one region still only grants one counter (assuming the condition is "kill 2+ enemies in one region").

### Dial Instructions

When a dial instruction is revealed (during the Advance Threat Dials step), it must be carried out. If conditions make it impossible, carry out as much as possible.

- **Draw X Chaos Cards:** Draw that many cards into your hand.
- **Place X Nobles:** Take X Noble tokens from the stockpile and place them in any region(s).
- **Place X Chaos Stone:** Take X Chaos Stone tokens from the stockpile and place them in any region(s).
- **[Power] Start:** Marks the starting position of that Power's dial. No other effect.
- **[Power] Victory!:** That Power wins the game (subject to tie-breaking and game-end checks).
- **Remove X Corruption:** Remove X corruption tokens (of any Power or combination) from the board.
- **Remove X World Tokens:** Remove X World tokens (of any type) from the board, returning them to the stockpile.
- **Score X VP:** Score X victory points.
- **Upgrade Card:** Choose one of your unused upgrade cards and place it into play.

## World Cards

Italicized text on a World Card, carried out as soon as the card is drawn.

### Persistent Effects

Non-italicized text, active while the card remains on the World Track. Persistent effects trigger:
- **During the "Resolve World Cards" step** of the End Phase (default), or
- At the **specified time** if the card says so (e.g., "At the beginning of the battle phase…" or "While this card remains in play…").

## World Tokens

Tokens introduced primarily through World Cards (but also from dial instructions and Chaos cards). The supply of each type is fixed — if a type runs out, no more are placed.

| Token | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Event** | No effect alone. Marks regions affected by certain World Card persistent effects. If the defining card leaves the track, Event tokens remain but have no effect until redefined. |
| **Hero** | In the End Phase, forces the highest-Threat player with figures in the region to remove one figure. |
| **Noble** | Increases the region's **Conquest Value** by 1. (Does not affect Resistance.) |
| **Peasant** | Available as battle targets. Some World Cards reward players who have claimed them. |
| **Ratmen** | Decreases the region's **Resistance** by 1. (Does not affect Conquest Value.) |
| **Chaos Stone** | Counts as 1 corruption token when determining whether a region is ruined. |

---

## Ruined Regions

Once a region has a Ruination Card (faceup or facedown):
- **No new Chaos cards** may be played to that region.
- **No new World tokens** may be placed in that region (existing tokens are not removed at time of ruination).
- **No VP are scored** for dominating that region.
- **No corruption tokens** are placed in that region.

All other normal rules remain in effect: battle still occurs, adjacency is unchanged, and the region still holds its place in Region Order.

---

## Control of Enemy Figures

When a player gains **control** of an enemy figure via a card effect, that figure is treated as if it were the controlling player's color for the duration of the effect. The controlling player:
- Rolls battle dice for it
- Places a corruption token of their color for it during the Corruption Phase
- etc.

The figure retains its **original statistics**, including any upgrades the original owner has unlocked.

---

## Upgrade Cards

Each Power has **5 upgrade cards**: 2 Chaos Power Upgrades and 3 Follower Upgrades.

Upgrade cards enter play when the **"Upgrade Card"** dial instruction is revealed. The player chooses any one of their not-yet-played upgrade cards and puts it into play.

- **Follower Upgrade:** Place over the corresponding pre-printed follower statistics on the Power Sheet. The upgrade's statistics and abilities replace those they cover.
- **Chaos Power Upgrade:** Place faceup adjacent to the Power Sheet, visible to all.

---

## Magic Symbols

Magic symbols appear on some Chaos cards (and can be granted to figures by other effects). They have **no effect on their own**, but are referenced by certain abilities — most notably Tenzen's dial advancement condition.

A figure that has been granted magic symbols "carries" those symbols to whichever region it occupies.

---

## Contradictory Effects

If a disabling effect and an enabling effect would apply to the same action simultaneously, the **disabling effect takes precedence**.

---

## Card Interaction Rulings

**The Altar + Heartgrinder upgrade:** When The Altar is active in a region, domination uses the sum of attack values instead of figure count. A Heartgrinder with the upgrade has an attack value of 4, so it contributes 4 to the sum — the "counts as 4 figures" wording on the upgrade is superseded by the attack value when The Altar is in effect.

**Threat ties for World Phase decisions:** Threat values across the four Powers are designed to avoid ties at their starting positions. If a tie occurs due to card or dial effects, the tied player earlier in Power Order decides.

---

## Player Count Notes

- **4 players:** All four Powers play. 7-card World Deck.
- **3 players:** Players choose which Power to omit. 8-card World Deck. Return that Power's components to the box.

In a 3-player game, the absent Power has no presence on the board and takes no actions. Its Threat Dial sits at its Start value for tie-breaking purposes.

## Upgrade Cards Reference

Each Power has five upgrade cards: two **Chaos Power Upgrades** and three **Follower Upgrades** (one per follower class). Stats are listed as **Cost / Attack / Defense**.

### Xarm Upgrade Cards

**Chaos Power Upgrades:**
- **Flood of Ferocity:** Draw one additional Chaos card in each draw phase.
- **Power of War:** When you recover power points in the draw phase, gain one power point more than the highlighted value.

**Follower Upgrades:**
- **Heartseekers Upgrade** *(Cost 1 / Attack 1 / Defense 1):* Heartseekers gain an attack value of 1.
- **Heartrippers Upgrade** *(Cost 2 / Attack 2 / Defense 1):* You may place one corruption token for each enemy figure your Heartrippers kill in the battle phase (including kills from explosions).
- **Heartgrinder Upgrade** *(Cost 3 / Attack 4 / Defense 3):* When calculating your domination value in a region, a single Heartgrinder counts as four figures.

---

### Gormen Upgrade Cards

**Chaos Power Upgrades:**
- **Provender of Rot:** Score 3 victory points each time any region is ruined.
- **Power of Decay:** When you recover power points in the draw phase, gain two power points more than the highlighted value.

**Follower Upgrades:**
- **Plaguecarriers Upgrade** *(Cost 1 / Attack 0 / Defense 1):* A Plaguecarrier's cost is 0 if placed in a region where you have no other figures. A given Plaguecarrier may only be summoned for free once per round.
- **Plaguebringers Upgrade** *(Cost 1 / Attack 1 / Defense 1):* Each Plaguebringer counts as two figures toward your domination value in any region it occupies.
- **Plaguefather Upgrade** *(Cost 3 / Attack 3 / Defense 3):* Immediately place two corruption tokens in a region when you summon your Plaguefather there.

---

### Tenzen Upgrade Cards

**Chaos Power Upgrades:**
- **Flood of Magic:** Draw to six Chaos cards in each draw phase instead of five.
- **Power of Fate:** When you recover power points in the draw phase, gain one power point more than the highlighted value.

**Follower Upgrades:**
- **Apprentices Upgrade** *(Cost 1 / Attack 0 / Defense 1):* When summoned from the board (rather than from your stockpile), an Apprentice may bring one Chaos Stone token (if present) from its origin region to its destination region.
- **Fractals Upgrade** *(Cost 2 / Attack 1 / Defense 2):* When you play a Chaos card with a magic symbol, you may immediately summon a Fractal to that region for 0 power points.
- **Fatespinner Upgrade** *(Cost 3 / Attack 3 / Defense 2):* Your Fatespinner counts as two Chaos Stone tokens when determining whether a region is ruined and when calculating magic symbol counts for your dial advancement condition.

---

### Lasha Upgrade Cards

**Chaos Power Upgrades:**
- **Power of Pain:** When you recover power points in the draw phase, gain one power point more than the highlighted value. If you also have **Power of Pleasure** in play, gain three points more (in total) instead.
- **Power of Pleasure:** When you recover power points in the draw phase, gain one power point more than the highlighted value. If you also have **Power of Pain** in play, gain three points more (in total) instead.

**Follower Upgrades:**
- **Addicts Upgrade** *(Cost 1 / Attack 0 / Defense 2):* Addicts gain a defense value of 2.
- **Ravers Upgrade** *(Cost 2 / Attack 1 / Defense 2):* Ravers also place corruption tokens during the corruption phase, as if they were Cultists.
- **The Rapture Upgrade** *(Cost 3 / Attack 2 / Defense 4):* At the beginning of the battle phase, choose any enemy Cultist or Warrior in this region. You control it until the end of the round or until The Rapture is killed, whichever comes first.

## Glossary

**Adjacent:** Sharing a border with another region.

**Battle dice:** Standard six-sided dice used to resolve combat.

**Chaos Card:** A card belonging to a specific Power used to place effects in regions and carry out special activities.

**Conquest Value:** How rewarding it is to dominate a region (VP scored upon domination).

**Corruption Token:** Tokens placed in regions by Cultists, representing how much a Power has defiled the area.

**Cultist:** The weakest class of follower. Attack value 0; cannot roll dice in battle, but places corruption tokens.

**Dial Advancement Counter:** A counter placed on a Threat Dial each time its Power fulfills its advancement condition.

**Domination Value:** Sum of Chaos card costs plus quantity of figures in a region.

**Explosion:** A battle die roll of 6, granting one additional die to roll.

**Follower:** Any plastic figure — Cultist, Warrior, or Incarnation.

**Incarnation:** The strongest class of follower.

**Hit:** A battle die result of 4, 5, or 6 that can damage an enemy figure.

**Magic Symbol:** An icon on certain Chaos cards (or granted by other effects) referenced by specific abilities.

**Populous:** A region tag. Some game effects specifically target Populous regions.

**Power Order:** Xarm → Gormen → Tenzen → Lasha.

**Power Points:** Currency spent to summon figures and play Chaos cards.

**Region Order:** Norrsund → Talvimaa → Volensk → Mittelreich → Normagne → Almerida → Toscavar → Pelascos → The Barrens.

**Resistance:** How difficult it is to dominate a region (must be strictly exceeded, not merely equaled).

**Ruination:** When a region's total corruption tokens (including Chaos Stone) reach 12+, it is ruined.

**Summon:** To place a follower on a region (from reserve or from another region on the board).

**Threat:** A numerical value shown on a Power's Threat Dial, used for tie-breaking and priority.

**Tick:** A single clockwise advancement of a Threat Dial.

**Tipped:** A figure that has received enough hits to be killed is tipped on its side and removed at end of battle resolution for that region.

**Upgrade Card:** A card that permanently improves a Power's abilities or follower statistics.

**Victory Point (VP):** Earned primarily through domination and ruination. Reaching 50 VP is one path to victory.

**Warrior:** A mid-tier class of follower. High combat value relative to cost.

**World Card:** Cards describing events in the mortal world. Draw one per round during the World Phase.

**World Token:** Tokens representing forces of the mortal world (Event, Hero, Noble, Peasant, Ratmen, Chaos Stone).
